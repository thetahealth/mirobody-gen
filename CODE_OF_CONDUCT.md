# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. Participants are expected to be respectful, to accept constructive criticism, and to
keep discussion on the work. Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to
developer@thetahealth.ai; reports are handled confidentially.
