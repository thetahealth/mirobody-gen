Drop manifest.jsonl here

Or click the button in the top right to pick a file. You can also drop in llm_panel.json (the multi-model scores) and a previously exported decisions.json.

What this is for. The deterministic audit (audit/clinical.py) catches identities, physiological bounds and demographic conflicts — questions with a definite answer. The multi-model panel (harness/llm_panel.py) produces a ranking. But whether a record reads like a real chart is, in the end, a human judgment.

How to use it. Pick a person on the left, step through visits in the middle, enter a verdict on the right. Keyboard is faster: J/K next/previous visit, 1/2/3 verdict, N write a note, [/] next/previous person.

Why every row carries a sparkline. Whether one value is correct, a rule checks better than a human can; a human's real edge is judging whether a whole curve looks like a real course of disease. The pale band in each sparkline is the reference range.
